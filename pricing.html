Frequently Asked Questions

Planning an elopement doesn't have to feel overwhelming.

What makes elopement planning feel overwhelming?

Most couples don't know where to start. Choosing a location, understanding permits, building a timeline, coordinating travel, and finding trusted vendors can feel like a lot when you're planning something entirely your own. The good news is you don't have to figure it all out by yourselves.

Why does eloping feel uncertain in the beginning?

Because there isn't a traditional wedding template to follow. Many couples wonder if they're doing things right or worry about overlooking something important. In reality, elopements are about creating an experience that reflects your relationship, not following a checklist.

What are couples most worried about on the day?

Weather, timing, and logistics are usually at the top of the list. Mountain conditions can change quickly, and unfamiliar locations can feel intimidating. Thoughtful planning and a flexible timeline help create a day that feels calm, relaxed, and fully present.

Do couples feel pressure about eloping instead of having a traditional wedding?

Sometimes. Family expectations and outside opinions can make the decision feel complicated. But many couples find that once they focus on what matters most to them, the path forward becomes much clearer and far more meaningful.

What logistics can be tricky?

Permits, travel coordination, weather planning, vendor recommendations, and choosing the right location are often the biggest hurdles. Working with someone who knows the area can simplify the process and help you focus on the experience instead of the stress.
